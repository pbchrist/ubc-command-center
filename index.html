What gets into Prospect Master

The Command Center is the broader intelligence pool. Prospect Master is the handoff list. A new ICONIC row is added only when the company is not Paused, has a named decision-maker, is not already in Prospect Master, and its source-backed sales case passes the support check. A verified email or direct phone is valuable, but is not required to preserve a legitimate prospect; missing contact data stays explicitly labeled for follow-up research.
