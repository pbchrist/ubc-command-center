How it gets sharper every night

Each rule below was learned from a real mistake and is now checked automatically on every run.

L-001Every fact carries its source page, the exact quote it rests on, and the date the page was read.Sept 2026: figures with no traceable source reached a report.
L-002Every number or date stated in a fact must appear in that fact's quote.Sept 2026: a figure was attached to the wrong company.
L-003A quote must be found word for word in the saved copy of its source page. A search summary is a lead, never a source.Sept 2026: a search summary gave a project's cost wrong.
L-004A fact belongs to the entity its quote names. Never move a number or event to a different company.Sept 2026: a fact was credited to the wrong company.
L-005Call something new or recent only if it is dated inside the reporting window. Older events are labelled background.Sept 2026: old items were presented as fresh.
L-006One value per fact. When sources disagree, show the conflict as unresolved instead of picking one.Sept 2026: one fee was shown two different ways.
L-007Never quote prices or make promises on the client's behalf. Proposals need the client's leadership to agree.Sept 2026: a report made a promise nobody had agreed to.
L-008An event that has ended is mentioned only as ended, never as a live opportunity.Sept 2026: advice rested on an offer that had already expired.
L-009Compare like with like. A comparison between different kinds of things is not a finding.Sept 2026: two different kinds of things were compared as if alike.
L-010A source that could not be read is held and reported, not treated as absent and not filled from memory.Sept 2026: one site was checked and 'no evidence' was claimed.
L-011Inference is labelled as inference and never stated as fact.Built in from the start: our reading is always labelled as our reading.
L-012No person, title, email or phone appears unless a cited page shows it. Never generate one because it looks plausible.Added Sept 2026: no contact detail appears without a page that shows it.
L-013Write for the client: plain words, and nothing about how the work is produced.Sept 2026: a report read like a machine wrote it.
L-014Nothing reaches a client without Patrick's approval, and one switch controls all sending.Added Sept 2026: one switch controls everything that is sent.
L-015Re-read every cited page on the morning it is shown or sent, and hold anything that changed.Sept 2026: source pages changed overnight before a report went out.
L-016The checker is a different AI from the writer.Added Sept 2026: a second, different AI checks every fact.
L-017Fetch before calling anything missing or unsaved; a tool's blind spot is not a fact about the world.Sept 2026: something was called missing when the check simply could not see it.
L-018A number counts only as the thing its own sentence says it is. A count of units, visitors or square feet is not a count of jobs.Sept 30, 2026, this system's first nightly run: a count of robots was read as new jobs.
